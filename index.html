Project Overview

What is this project?

This project delivers a fully interactive Power BI dashboard for an insurance company to understand its policyholder base, claim patterns, and risk exposure — all from a single, centralized view.

🎯 Business Problem

Policy and claims data were scattered across multiple sources, making it nearly impossible for stakeholders to track performance or identify trends efficiently.

💡 The Solution

A centralized Power BI dashboard with dual-view toggle (Claim Amount / Total Policies), 5 KPI cards, and 8 dynamic charts — all cross-filtering in real time.

📊 The Impact

Stakeholders can now identify high-risk segments, track geographic exposure, and profile customers by demographics — all in a single click.

⚙️ The Architecture

Power Query for data transformation, DAX for calculated measures, and Field Parameters for the dynamic dual-view — a production-ready, scalable BI solution.

What We Did

End-to-End Build Process

From raw spreadsheet to interactive executive dashboard — here's how the project was built, step by step.

Data Ingestion & Power Query Transformation

Loaded raw Excel dataset into Power BI. Cleaned inconsistent BirthDate formats, created Age Group bins (15-25 through 66-75), corrected data types across all 16 columns, and handled null values in Claim Freq and Kids Driving.

Data Modeling

Built a clean flat-table model with proper data types enabling correct aggregation. Configured filter propagation and relationship settings to support all planned cross-filtering interactions.

DAX Measure Development

Created all 5 core KPI measures explicitly in DAX — Total Claim Amount (SUM), Total Policies (COUNTROWS), Average Claim Amount (AVERAGE), Claim Frequency (AVERAGE), and Gender-wise splits using CALCULATE with FILTER.

Dynamic Dual-View via Field Parameters

Implemented Power BI Field Parameters to create a single toggle button that switches all 8 chart Y-axes between Total Claim Amount and Total Policies simultaneously — no duplicate pages required.

Dashboard Design & Layout

Designed an 8-chart layout across two toggle views: Donut (Car Use, Coverage Zone), Bar (Car Make), Histogram (Age Group), Area (Car Year), Ribbon (Kids Driving), Pie (Education), and Matrix Heat Grid (Education × Marital Status). KPI cards in a dedicated left panel with a consistent color theme.

Vehicle Risk

  • $150.4M from private vehicles (80% of all claims)
  • Ford is highest-risk brand at $17M total
  • Claims peak for vehicles manufactured 2000–2018
  • Commercial vehicles account for only $37.4M

Demographics

  • Age group 46–55 has highest claims ($36M) and policies (7.1K)
  • 15–25 bracket has lowest volume but higher per-policy risk
  • Near-equal gender split: 18.8K female vs 18.7K male
  • Bachelors holders: 19K policies, $94M in claims

Geography

  • All 5 zones remarkably even at ~$37–38M each
  • Urban leads at $38.23M; Highly Rural lowest at $37.26M
  • Zone is not a strong differentiator for claim severity
  • Policy distribution mirrors claim distribution geographically

Education × Marital

  • Single + High School highest segment: $40.2M
  • Single + Bachelors close second at $38.7M
  • Married policyholders show stable, moderate behavior
  • PhD holders lowest risk across all statuses: $13.69M
